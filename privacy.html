Privacy Policy

At Universal Union Logistics, we value your trust and are deeply committed to protecting your personal data and commercial consignment records. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information across all digital and logistical touchpoints.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal details such as sender and receiver names, telephone numbers, delivery addresses, email addresses, and consignment tracking numbers necessary to fulfill freight delivery, customs brokerage, and live shipment telemetry.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized exclusively for operational logistics purposes, including dispatch routing, real-time shipment updates, customs document verification, automated transit notifications, and client support inquiries.

3. Data Protection & Security

We enforce industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption, database firewalls, and role-based administrative access controls to safeguard your personal and shipment data from unauthorized access or alteration.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Carriers

We never sell or rent your personal data to commercial advertisers. We only share consignment details with authorized air, ocean, and ground transportation partners strictly necessary to complete cross-border delivery.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to your historical shipment records, correct inaccurate account details, or request data deletion upon consignment completion. Contact our compliance officer at contact@universalunionlogistics.com.
