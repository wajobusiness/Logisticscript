Terms & Conditions

Please review the following contractual terms carefully. By booking a consignment or utilizing Universal Union Logistics's shipping, forwarding, and tracking services, you agree to abide by these terms.

1. Service Overview

Universal Union Logistics provides international freight transportation, customs brokerage, parcel forwarding, and warehousing services. All shipments are subject to air and maritime carrier regulations, tariff declarations, and the rules specified herein.

2. Sender Responsibilities

The sender must furnish precise consignee information, genuine parcel descriptions, accurate declarations of cargo value, and appropriate packaging to withstand normal transit stresses. Senders are responsible for any penalties resulting from misdeclared goods.

3. Prohibited & Hazardous Goods

We strictly prohibit the carriage of illegal narcotics, unlicensed firearms, corrosive chemicals, combustible explosives, or any items embargoed by international aviation or maritime treaties. Violations will result in cargo confiscation and referral to regulatory authorities.

4. Liability & Cargo Insurance

While we maintain the highest security and handling protocols, standard liability for un-insured freight is governed by standard Warsaw/Montreal and Hague-Visby rules. Optional comprehensive all-risk cargo insurance is available upon booking.

5. Delivery Attempts & Unclaimed Cargo

Our couriers make up to three physical delivery attempts. Parcels unclaimed after 14 calendar days at local bonded warehouses may incur daily demurrage or storage fees.
